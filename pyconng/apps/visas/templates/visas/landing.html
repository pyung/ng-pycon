Who can have one

  • Anyone holding a paid ticket.
  • Speakers whose proposal has been accepted.
  • Travel grant recipients, from the moment the grant is offered.
  • The organizing team and volunteers.

We confirm this from your records rather than asking you to tell us, because the letter says it on our letterhead to a foreign government.
